# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

**Firmware update for sena** (package `com.intercom.update`) is an unofficial, open-source app by demus9. It is not affiliated with Sena Technologies.

## Data collection

The app does **not collect, store or share personal data**. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it sends no information about you or your device to the developer or any third party.

## Network access

The app uses the internet only to download the firmware list and firmware files from Sena's public server (`https://firmware.sena.com/`). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to that server. The app does not control Sena's server or its logging; see Sena's own privacy policy for that.

## Local data

- Firmware files you choose or download are copied to the app's private cache, used for the update, and are not sent anywhere. They can be removed by clearing the app's storage.
- The app reads USB device identifiers (vendor ID, product ID, firmware version reply) of the connected headset to decide whether an update is possible. This information stays on your device.

## Links to outside services

The "Support the developer" button and the "Contact developer" button open `buymeacoffee.com` and `github.com` in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The "Contact developer" button prefills a GitHub issue with the USB ID or firmware version in question, and nothing is sent unless you submit the issue yourself.
